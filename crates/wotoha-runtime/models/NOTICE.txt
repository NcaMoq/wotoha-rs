Wotoha offline AutoMix beat-analysis assets

The two ONNX files below are embedded into the executable with `include_bytes!`
and are loaded without network or filesystem access at runtime. The release
archives intentionally do not duplicate these approximately 10 MiB model
bytes: the executable is the distributed model copy, while this notice and
the complete upstream license texts are shipped in `third-party/neural-models`.

That script downloads official Beat This! checkpoints from the JKU cloud and
exports the ONNX assets. The original implementation and published weights
state their MIT basis and copyright here:
https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this/blob/b95c8ab0c58c2d9fcfd40508ae8dffbc05ac4f5c/README.md#license

and that text is retained in `LICENSE.beat-this-original.txt`.
The corresponding reference paper is "Beat This! Accurate Beat Tracking
Without DBN Postprocessing" (ISMIR 2024): https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21658

## Copyright and license basis

The embedded weights are published Beat This! model outputs, not training
datasets. CPJKU's primary README says that the code and published model
weights are released under MIT, while warning that some training files have
separate terms; no training files or datasets are redistributed here. The
MIT copyright attribution for the original work is:

    Copyright (c) 2024 Institute of Computational Perception, JKU Linz, Austria

The pinned beat-this-rs port retains that attribution and adds:

    Copyright (c) 2025 danigb (Rust port)

The complete pinned-port MIT text (including both notices) is
`LICENSE.beat-this-rs.txt`; the complete original MIT text is
`LICENSE.beat-this-original.txt`. `embedded_rten.rs` is a small derivative of
the pinned port's `src/runtime/rten.rs` and is covered by the port's MIT terms.
